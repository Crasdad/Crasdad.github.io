TradeCalc — Privacy Policy (Draft)

Last revised: October 25, 2026

Pre-release draft: This policy has not yet been verified against the release Android App Bundle. It must be finalized before it is used for a public Google Play listing.

About TradeCalc

TradeCalc (Android application identifier com.crasdad.tradecalc) is a construction calculation app published by its developer. The app provides on-device calculators and may remember calculation settings or history on your device.

Information and local storage

TradeCalc is designed to perform construction calculations on your device without an account. Calculation entries and preferences may be stored locally for continuity of use. Removing app storage or uninstalling the app generally removes this locally stored information, subject to device backup settings.

Advertising and analytics — verification pending

The developer is verifying whether the release build contains active advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. If such services are enabled, they may process technical information such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, app interactions, diagnostics, and network information according to their own policies. This section will be updated with the specific service providers and data practices before release. Do not treat this draft as the completed data disclosure.

Data sharing, security, and retention

Calculator entries are intended to stay on the device and are not intended for sale. Locally stored app information is retained until removed by the user or by the operating system. Any third-party transmission, retention, and deletion practices must be confirmed after the release-build review. No method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed perfectly secure.

Children

TradeCalc is intended as a general-purpose construction utility, not specifically for children.

Changes to this policy

The developer will update this page when data practices change, including before activating additional advertising or analytics services.

Privacy questions

For privacy questions or requests, please use the developer contact form on GitHub. A GitHub account may be required to submit an issue.

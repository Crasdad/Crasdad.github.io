TradeCalc — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy describes TradeCalc for Android (com.crasdad.tradecalc), version 1.0.0.

Calculations and local information

TradeCalc performs construction calculations on your device without requiring an account. The app does not provide cloud synchronization or a service for submitting your calculation records to the developer.

For its calculator and saved-history features, TradeCalc stores your favorite calculators, appearance preference, and up to 30 recent calculations in Android app-private storage. Recent entries can include calculator type, entered numbers and choices, calculation time, and a randomly generated local record ID. The record ID identifies a saved calculation, not an account or advertising identity.

Stored information is protected by Android app-storage controls; TradeCalc does not add its own encryption layer. Protection depends on your operating system, device security, and access to your device.

Retention and deleting local data

TradeCalc retains up to 30 recent calculations. Adding further entries replaces the oldest; there is no time-based automatic deletion. Favorites and appearance preferences remain until changed, reset, or removed with app storage.

In Settings, Clear recent calculations removes recent history while preserving favorites and appearance preferences. Reset saved data restores local history, favorites, and preferences to defaults. These actions depend on successful storage writes. You can also clear TradeCalc storage through Android settings or uninstall the app. These methods are not guaranteed forensic erasure and cannot remove copies previously copied, shared, or retained through device or operating-system services.

Advertising, analytics, identifiers and diagnostics

TradeCalc version 1.0.0 does not display advertisements, use Android's Advertising ID, or integrate an active third-party analytics or remote crash-reporting service. No developer-operated backend for collecting calculations, preferences, or user profiles is included in this version. Android, Google Play and device services may separately process information according to their own policies and settings.

Copy and share

When you choose Copy, TradeCalc places result text on your device clipboard. Your clipboard history, keyboard, synchronization features, or other software may retain that text. When you choose Share, Android opens its share chooser so that you can choose a receiving app or recipient. Other apps and recipients handle anything you choose to send under their own policies. TradeCalc cannot delete copies held by those third parties.

Backup and transfer

TradeCalc disables standard Android app backup and does not offer its own cloud backup service. Device-to-device transfer and manufacturer or operating-system services can behave differently, and the app cannot guarantee that every migration mechanism excludes locally stored data.

Visiting this page and external links

This privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting the website involves your browser and GitHub's hosting services, whose handling of technical information is separate from TradeCalc's local calculations. Opening external links from TradeCalc takes you outside the app, subject to the destination's practices.

Privacy questions

You may contact the developer through the TradeCalc policy repository's public GitHub issue channel. Do not post passwords, private calculations, or other sensitive personal information there; issues are public and GitHub sign-in may be required. GitHub handles submissions under its privacy statement.

Policy changes

We may update this policy if TradeCalc's data practices or features change. The updated page will identify its effective date.
